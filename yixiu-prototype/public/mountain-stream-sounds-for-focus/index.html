A three-step focus setup

Give the task a quiet edge

You do not need a new system. Make the next action visible, keep the stream below attention and let the timer hold the boundary.

  1. 01

    Open the work first

    Put the page, problem or paragraph in front of you before pressing play. A concrete starting point removes one more decision.

  2. 02

    Set the lowest useful volume

    Use enough sound to soften the room, but not so much that individual splashes pull you away from the task.

  3. 03

    Keep one interval intact

    Work until the timer ends. If your attention wanders, return to the next visible action instead of changing sounds.

Choosing a focus sound

Why a stream instead of music?

Music can bring melody, language and memory into the room. A continuous stream offers variation without asking you to follow a song.

  1. 01

    For reading and writing

    There are no lyrics or spoken prompts to overlap with the words you are trying to understand or create.

  2. 02

    For a bright daytime room

    The open, detailed texture can feel more present than soft rain while remaining less forceful than a musical playlist.

  3. 03

    For repeatable sessions

    Using the same sound at the start of a task can become a simple personal cue: the stream begins, and the work begins.

Before you begin

Mountain stream sounds: common questions

Is this mountain stream mixed with music or talking?

No. The preview is flowing-water ambience without music, lyrics or spoken guidance.

What kind of work suits a mountain stream sound?

Its detailed but continuous texture can suit reading, writing, study and other tasks where you want a natural sound behind the work.

How loud should I play the stream?

Use the lowest comfortable level that softens nearby distractions. The stream should sit behind your task rather than become the task.

Can the stream keep playing while I use another iPhone app?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the selected sound can continue while you read, write or use another iPhone app.
