How to use nature sounds for focus

The sound should support the task, not become the task. Pick one texture, keep the volume low and give the session a clear edge.

  1. 01

    Match the sound to the room

    Use Ocean Waves for a slow, even rhythm; Mountain Stream for a brighter texture; or Bamboo Rain when voices and small interruptions keep breaking through.

  2. 02

    Choose one work interval

    Start with 30 minutes for reading, writing or study. Let the timer define the session so you do not need to keep checking the clock.

  3. 03

    Let Yixiu move to the background

    Turn on background playback, open the document in front of you and leave the sound unchanged until the timer ends.

Before you begin

Focus sounds: common questions

Which nature sound should I use for focus?

Start with a steady sound that does not ask for your attention. Ocean Waves have a slow rise and fall, while Mountain Stream and Bamboo Rain provide a more detailed texture.

Can Yixiu keep playing while I use another iPhone app?

Yes. Turn on background playback and the selected nature sound can continue while you read, write or use another app.

Does Yixiu include a focus timer?

Yes. Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Yixiu Plus also includes unlimited listening.

Does Yixiu play music or spoken guidance?

The focus soundscapes use nature audio without songs, lyrics or spoken guidance, so the sound can remain in the background.
