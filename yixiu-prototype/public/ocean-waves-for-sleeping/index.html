Before you press play

Ocean waves for sleeping: common questions

Do these ocean waves include music or talking?

No. The preview contains ocean-wave ambience without music, lyrics, narration or a spoken sleep story.

Are ocean waves steady enough for bedtime?

Ocean waves rise, break and return in a repeating rhythm. Preview the strongest break at a low volume first. If each wave keeps taking your attention, choose a steadier rain recording instead.

How loud should ocean waves be while sleeping?

Use the lowest level that gently changes the room and still lets you hear an alarm, another person or anything important around you. Avoid placing a speaker directly beside your head.

Can the waves continue when I lock my iPhone?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the ocean scene can continue when the screen is locked or you leave the app. You can also set a 15, 30 or 60 minute timer.
