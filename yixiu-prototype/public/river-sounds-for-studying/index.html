A simple study ritual

Begin before motivation arrives

The river is not a promise of perfect concentration. It is a small, repeatable cue that helps you enter one defined block of work.

  1. 01

    Name the finish line

    Choose a result small enough for one block: five pages, three problems or one paragraph revised.

  2. 02

    Remove one interruption

    Silence one notification source and put the next action on screen before you start the river.

  3. 03

    Keep the sound in the background

    If you begin listening to the water itself, lower the volume. The task should remain the clearest thing in the room.

Choosing a study sound

River sound, rain or silence?

There is no universally best background. Choose the quietest option that helps you begin and keeps language out of the way.

  1. 01

    Use a river for gentle movement

    A stream changes from moment to moment without becoming a melody, which can suit long reading and writing sessions.

  2. 02

    Use rain for a softer room

    Rain is more even and enclosed. Try it when you want fewer bright details in the sound around you.

  3. 03

    Use silence when it already works

    Background sound is optional. If silence feels comfortable and interruptions are low, keep the setup simple.

Before you study

River sounds: common questions

Are these river sounds mixed with music or talking?

No. The sound is flowing-water ambience without music, lyrics, spoken guidance or a voiceover.

Can river sounds help me build a study routine?

A consistent sound can become a simple starting cue. Open one assignment, start the river and keep the same sound until the study block ends.

What volume should I use while studying?

Choose the lowest comfortable volume that softens nearby distractions. You should be able to read without tracking individual splashes.

Can Yixiu keep playing while I read in another iPhone app?

Yes. Enable background playback in Yixiu, choose a timer and continue reading or writing in another app.
