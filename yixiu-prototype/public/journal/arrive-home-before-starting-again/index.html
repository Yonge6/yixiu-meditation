A small pause2 min read

Arrive home before starting again

Your keys are down, but your attention may still be on the journey. Leave a little room before the next thing.

Set down what you carried

Put your keys in their usual place, take off your coat and settle anything that needs immediate attention. You do not have to plan the whole evening now. If someone needs you as you arrive, this pause can wait until a space opens.

Give unfinished thoughts a place

If a work message is still on your mind, write what you need to check tomorrow. This is not an instruction to change your mood instantly. You are giving the thing you want to remember a place outside a repeating mental conversation.

Choose an optional transition sound

Free Spring Creek can accompany a short sit. Check whether others nearby want to hear it and choose a suitable volume. Or leave the player off and listen to the home itself. The pause needs no purchase and no prescribed posture.

Choose one evening activity

Next might be cooking, washing or speaking with someone at home. Choose what fits now rather than counting everything unfinished. Arriving can be a very short transition. You do not have to complete a sequence before the evening can belong to you.
