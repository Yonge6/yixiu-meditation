Rest without a streak

A minute today does not have to repay the days you were away.

Nothing to make up

If you have not opened Yixiu for a few days, other things may simply have needed your time. Returning does not require making up minutes or explaining the gap. Rest is not homework with a balance carried into the next session.

Use the time you actually have

Choose the free one-minute option in Focus if that is the time available. Start when ready and keep breathing naturally and comfortably, without chasing the animation or forcing a hold. If following a rhythm does not appeal, simply sit for a moment.

Read the record as a reference

Completed sessions stay in local records. They can remind you of time you set aside, but they are not a daily score to deliver. Web records belong to this browser and do not sync automatically across devices; an empty new browser does not erase your actual breaks.

No promise about tomorrow required

When the minute ends, return to the next part of today. You may want to come back tomorrow or leave it unplanned. Finishing a short practice does not require committing to a long streak. This moment needs no extra proof.
