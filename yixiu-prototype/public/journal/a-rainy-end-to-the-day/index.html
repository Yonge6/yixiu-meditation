A rainy end to the day

One less thing to finish. A little rain, and a few minutes of your own.

Leave the next thing for tomorrow

If you are still moving between tabs, pause here. Write down one thing you want to remember tomorrow, then choose a sound. Tonight does not need another task to complete, or a deadline for falling asleep.

Start with a little rain

Rain on Eaves is a free selection in Yixiu's web experience. Use the button below and start at a comfortable low volume. Let it be part of the room rather than something that has to cover every sound. If rain is not for you, try Ocean Waves.

Give this moment an ending

Tap the duration in the player and choose 5, 15 or 30 minutes. The sound winds down when the timer ends. Start with five minutes if you are just trying it; you can pause whenever you want.

Try the web, keep the app close

The web experience needs no account. If you want to continue on iPhone, open Yixiu, check background playback and choose a timer. The app has free selections and optional Plus features. You can listen first before deciding what fits your routine.
