Rain, waves or music? No winner required

Try sounds with the same small task. What fits today does not have to become a permanent preference.

Use a real task

Try a short passage you need to read or a small organizing job. Scene names alone cannot tell you what you will enjoy, and you do not need a sound that works for everyone. Ask whether it fits the thing in front of you now.

Change one thing at a time

Listen to Ocean Waves at a comfortable volume, then try Rain on Eaves with the same task. There is no need to change volume and timer at the same time. Notice whether you keep abandoning the task to listen; lower the sound or try silence if you do.

Music is another option

Oasis Rest, Ocean Passage and First Breath are free to try on the web. Melody feels different from nature sound, but neither is a more advanced choice. If you want to follow the music closely, keep it for a break rather than forcing it into the background.

Keep one choice for today

Once something fits, stop comparing, save it as a favorite and return to the task. You can choose again when your surroundings or work change. There is no ranking to complete, and leaving the player off is a valid result of the comparison.
