Before bed2 min read

Make a little room beside the bed

No room makeover required. Put a few things away, choose a sound and give it an ending.

Only what is within reach

Set your cup somewhere steady, mark your page and move the charging cable out of the way. You can stop after these few things. This does not need to become a room-cleaning project or a checklist you must finish before resting.

Choose before putting the screen down

If you want rain tonight, open Rain on Eaves, set a comfortable low volume and choose a duration. The free web player offers 5, 15 and 30 minutes. Selecting the perfect sound does not need to become another endless browsing session.

Consider the shared room

If someone is nearby, ask whether they want to listen too. Sound does not have to fill the room or stay on throughout the evening. If your preferences differ, leaving it off tonight is an option, not a failed routine.

Enough preparation for now

The unfinished book and the rest of the desk can wait. Music is optional company, not a promise of sleep. Once you have chosen it, you do not have to keep checking whether you feel relaxed enough. Pause whenever you prefer.
