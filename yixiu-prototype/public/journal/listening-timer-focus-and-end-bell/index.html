Listening timer, Focus duration or end bell?

These three controls do different jobs. Choose the right one before starting your session.

Listening timers live in Sounds

Tap the duration above the play button or the clock icon to choose a session length. The free web options are 5, 15 and 30 minutes; 60 minutes and unlimited listening belong to app Plus. Choosing a duration still requires starting playback for the session.

Focus has a separate start button

The duration in the bottom Focus tab belongs to the breathing practice, not the Sounds listening timer. One minute is free on the web, while longer options explain app access. Select a duration and press Start; switching tabs alone does not complete a practice.

The end bell is optional

You can enable the end bell in My Yixiu; it is off by default. When enabled, it marks natural listening or Focus completion. Pausing, resetting or leaving early should not ring it. Audibility also depends on playback volume, the device and the current listening environment.

Use a system alarm for appointments

Neither the timer nor the cue is a reliable wake-up alarm. In particular, do not assume a background browser will sound at the required moment. Set and check a separate system alarm for anything time-critical, and use a short listening session to explore your preferences.
