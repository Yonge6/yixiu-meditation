Begin work with one small step

Finish the project is a large instruction. Open one file, change one sentence and let a stream stay in the background.

Turn an outcome into an action

Replace finish the proposal with open the document and list three questions. Make the instruction small enough to act on now, not another way of asking for everything at once. Put it on paper or at the top of the document and leave the scope there.

Keep only what this step needs

Set aside unrelated pages and note any links you need later. Your desk does not have to stay tidy forever; you are making room for this action. Mark unresolved questions for later and work on the part you can address now.

Keep sound in a supporting role

Mountain Stream is free to try in Yixiu on the web. Listen briefly, choose fifteen minutes and return to work if it suits you. Sound is optional background, not something that completes the task for you. Turn it off if it becomes another distraction.

Leave a place to return to

At the end, note where you stopped and the next concrete action. Even one edited paragraph can leave a useful handover to your future self. There is no need to start another timer immediately or dismiss the work because it was small.
