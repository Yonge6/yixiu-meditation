One sound, one page

Before reading or writing, make the next step a little smaller.

Choose one small thing

You do not have to finish the book or write the whole article now. Choose a specific beginning: two pages, three lines, or a short set of notes. Leave the other tasks until this small stretch is over.

Let the sound accompany you

Ocean Waves is a free sound in Yixiu on the web. Listen briefly and decide whether it fits this reading session. You might prefer its rise and fall, a little rain, or silence. There is no single right answer.

Try fifteen minutes

Choose a 15-minute timer in the player, then return to your book or document. The web experience needs no account and works as a listening option on computers and non-iPhone devices too. If the sound draws your attention away, lower the volume or pause.

Keep what works for you

Tap the heart if you would like to keep the current sound in your favorites. Web preferences stay in this browser and do not automatically follow you to another device. Next time, choose a familiar sound and begin with the page in front of you.
