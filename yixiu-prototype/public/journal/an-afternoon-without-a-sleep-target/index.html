An afternoon without a sleep target

A short lunch break can be a break from work, whether or not you fall asleep.

Leave yourself a next step

If afternoon work is still on your mind, write one concrete instruction for your return: open the estimate and check the second item. Leave the note on your desk and close the document. You do not have to rehearse the entire afternoon during your break.

Choose a little background

Try the free Oasis Rest track, or leave the room quiet. It is a short composition, not an original one-hour recording; the player timer sets your listening session. Begin at a low volume. If you do not enjoy it, pausing is a perfectly good choice.

Keep your alarm separate

If you have a meeting to return to, set and check a system alarm on your phone. The music timer ends playback, and the optional completion cue is not a dependable wake-up alarm. Browser background rules can also affect web playback.

Return without grading the break

Look outside for a moment, settle into your seat and return to the step you wrote down. There is no need to make up a practice because you did not sleep. You set aside a little time without work; there is no result to submit.
