Getting started2 min read

Keep your favorite sounds close

The heart remembers a preference in this browser. It is not a cloud backup, a download or a Plus purchase.

Listen before tapping the heart

Choose a sound you actually enjoy, then tap the heart in the player. Free First Breath is one option to try. You do not need to collect the entire library. Favorites are there to shorten the next decision, not create another listening checklist.

Find it in My Yixiu

Return in the same browser and open your favorites in My Yixiu to choose the sound again. Tap the player's heart once more to remove that preference. Removing a favorite does not delete the original recording from Yixiu.

Web preferences do not cloud-sync

Favorites and preferences stay in this browser without an account. They do not automatically follow you to another device. Clearing site data or using a temporary browsing session may remove them. This is not a cloud backup; note the names yourself if you want a separate reference.

A favorite does not change access

The heart is not an audio download and does not unlock Plus. The web player remains the free experience; Apple purchases and restoration are verified in the app. A different favorite list on another device is not a reason to buy membership again.
