Before you begin

Nature sounds for meditation: common questions

Which nature sound should I choose for meditation?

Try each sound briefly at a low volume. Keep the one that can sit at the edge of your attention: flowing water for bright movement, forest wind for open space, rain for a steadier texture or ocean waves for a slower rise and fall.

Does the page include music or guided talking?

No. The four previews contain nature sounds without songs, lyrics, narration, affirmations or spoken meditation instructions.

How loud should nature sounds be during meditation?

Start at the lowest level you can hear comfortably. If you are following every splash, bird call or wave, lower the volume until the sound can remain in the background.

Can I use a timer and keep the sound playing on iPhone?

Yes. The web preview has 15, 30 and 60-minute choices. In Yixiu for iPhone, background playback can keep your selected scene playing while you lock the screen or use another app.
