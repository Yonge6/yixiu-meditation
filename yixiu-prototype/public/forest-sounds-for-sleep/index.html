Forest sounds for sleep: common questions

What can I hear in this forest sound?

The preview uses a real outdoor recording of wind moving through a forest with distant bird calls. It is not a synthesized noise track or a musical forest mix.

Does the forest-sleep preview include music or talking?

No. The preview contains forest wind and distant birds without songs, lyrics, narration, stories or spoken sleep guidance.

Are bird calls too noticeable for bedtime?

The calls sit farther back than in Yixiu's morning-bird scene, but every listener is different. Preview the loudest moments at a low volume and choose rain, ocean waves or mountain wind if the birds keep drawing your attention.

Can forest sounds continue after I lock my iPhone?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the selected scene can continue when you lock the screen or leave the app. You can also set a 15, 30 or 60 minute timer.
