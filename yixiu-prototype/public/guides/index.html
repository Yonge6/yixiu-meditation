A quieter way to choose

Three decisions are enough

The sound library is not the task. Decide what this moment is for, set one boundary and stop browsing.

  1. 01

    Name the moment

    Sleep, study, work or pause. A concrete purpose makes the next choice smaller.

  2. 02

    Choose the lowest useful volume

    The sound should soften the room, not become the center of it.

  3. 03

    Set a finish line

    Use a 15-, 30- or 60-minute timer, or let one minute be enough.

Before you begin

Yixiu listening guides

Where should I start if I do not know which sound to choose?

Start with the moment, not the library. Choose rain for bedtime, a river or ocean for focused work, or the one-minute reset when you only need a brief pause.

Do the Yixiu previews include music or talking?

The nature-sound previews use water, rain or ocean ambience without music or spoken guidance. The breathing reset adds a simple visual pace rather than a voiceover.

Can I listen before downloading the iPhone app?

Yes. Each guide offers a free web preview. The iPhone app adds timers, favorites and background playback.
