Forest sounds for focus: common questions

What can I hear in this forest sound?

The preview uses a real outdoor recording with moving forest wind and distant bird calls. It is not a synthesized noise track or a musical forest mix.

Does the forest preview include music or talking?

No. There are no songs, lyrics, narration, spoken focus prompts or changing playlists in the preview.

How is this different from morning bird sounds?

This scene gives more space to wind moving through the forest, with bird calls farther back. The morning-bird scene has brighter and more noticeable individual calls.

Can the forest sound continue while I use another iPhone app?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the selected scene can continue while you read, write or use another app. You can also set a 15, 30 or 60 minute timer.
