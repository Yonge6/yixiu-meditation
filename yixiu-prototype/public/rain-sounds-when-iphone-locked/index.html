Rain sounds and the iPhone lock screen

Why do Apple's Background Sounds stop when I lock my iPhone?

In Settings → Accessibility → Audio & Visual → Background Sounds, check Stop Sounds When Locked. Turn it off if you want the built-in sound to continue while the iPhone is locked.

Do I need an app for Apple's built-in Background Sounds?

No. Background Sounds is included in iOS. If one of Apple's built-in sounds fits your needs, it is the quickest option and does not require another app.

Can Yixiu keep rain playing with the screen locked?

Yes. Enable background playback in Yixiu, start a rain scene and lock the screen. The sound can continue until you pause it or the selected timer finishes.

What does Yixiu add beyond the iPhone setting?
