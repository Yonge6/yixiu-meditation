A simple study setup

Give one task a quiet boundary

The sound is not the study session. It is only a steady edge around one defined piece of work. Make three decisions, then stop adjusting.

  1. 01

    Choose one visible task

    Open the problem set, chapter or document before pressing play. A concrete next action keeps the sound from becoming another thing to browse.

  2. 02

    Use the lowest useful volume

    Set the rain just high enough to soften nearby movement or conversation. Your work should remain more noticeable than individual drops.

  3. 03

    Set one finish line

    Complete one section or use a 15-, 30- or 60-minute timer. Keep the sound and volume unchanged until that boundary arrives.

Rain instead of a playlist

Choose by task, not by trend

There is no universal best background sound. The useful choice is the one that asks the least of your attention during this task.

  1. 01

    Try rain for language-heavy work

    Rain has no lyrics or narration, so it may suit reading, essay writing, revision or proofreading when another stream of words feels busy.

  2. 02

    Use silence when sound becomes the task

    If you are learning pronunciation, editing audio or reading aloud, pause the rain. Background sound should never make the material harder to hear.

  3. 03

    Keep the better condition

    Compare one short block with rain and one in silence. Choose the condition in which you notice the task more than the room.

Rain sounds for studying: common questions

Does this study rain include music, talking or thunder?

No. The preview is a real light-rain recording without music, lyrics, spoken guidance or added thunder.

What volume should I use while studying?

Use the lowest comfortable level that softens nearby distractions. The assignment should remain more noticeable than individual raindrops.

Is rain better than music for studying?

It depends on the person and task. Rain has no lyrics or melody to follow, which some people prefer for reading or writing, while others work best in silence.

Can the rain keep playing while I study in another iPhone app?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the selected rain scene can continue while you use notes, reading or study apps.
