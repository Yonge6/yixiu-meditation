A short bedtime test

Choose once, then stop adjusting

The comparison is useful only if it returns you to rest. Use the same volume and the same room for each short listen.

  1. 01

    Start quieter than expected

    Set the volume just high enough to hear the texture. The recording does not need to fill the room.

  2. 02

    Notice what pulls you back

    If you keep waiting for the next wave, drop or rumble, try a more even option.

  3. 03

    Keep one for the timer

    Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes and leave the sound unchanged for that session.

Common questions

Choosing a sleep sound

What is the best sound to play for sleep?

There is no single sound that fits everyone. Compare a few at low volume and keep the one you notice least once you settle into bed.

Is rain or white noise better at bedtime?

Rain has natural variation, while underwater white noise is more even. Try each at the same quiet volume and choose by how easily it can fade into the room.

Should a sleep sound include music or talking?

That is a personal choice. These previews contain nature recordings without music or speech so you can compare their textures directly.

Can the sound continue when my iPhone is locked?

The free web previews play while the page remains open. Yixiu for iPhone adds background playback for continuing a selected scene after you lock the screen or switch apps.
