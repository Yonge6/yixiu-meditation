Wind, rain or ocean for bedtime?

No sound is best for every listener. The useful choice is the one that fades into the room instead of repeatedly pulling you back toward the speaker.

  1. 01

    Wind for wide, changing air

    Choose mountain wind when you like a spacious texture that slowly rises and falls. It can make a closed room feel less still without adding melody or speech.

  2. 02

    Use rain sounds for sleep when individual gusts feel too noticeable and you want a more even layer around the room.

  3. 03

    Try ocean waves when a clear rise, break and return feels easier to leave in the background than open mountain air.

Before you press play

Wind sounds for sleeping: common questions

Do these wind sounds include music or talking?

No. The preview is a natural mountain-wind recording without music, lyrics, narration or a spoken sleep story.

Are wind sounds steady enough for bedtime?

Wind rises and falls more than light rain. Preview it at a low volume first. If the gusts keep taking your attention, choose a steadier rain or ocean recording instead.

How loud should wind sounds be while sleeping?

Use the lowest level that gently changes the room and still lets you hear anything important around you. Avoid placing a speaker directly beside your head.

Can the wind continue when I lock my iPhone?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the selected scene can continue when the screen is locked or you leave the app. You can also set a 15, 30 or 60 minute timer.
