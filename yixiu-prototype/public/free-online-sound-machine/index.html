Before you begin

Online sound machine questions

Is this online sound machine free?

Yes. All 10 browser recordings and the 15-, 30- and 60-minute timer can be used without an account or ads.

Which sound should I choose?

There is no universal best sound. Try a few at the same low volume and keep the texture that can sit behind your room without asking for attention.

Will the sound continue when I lock my iPhone?

The web player works while the page remains open. Yixiu for iPhone adds background playback when you lock the screen or switch apps.

Does the timer stop the recording?

Yes. Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes and the active web recording stops when that timer reaches zero.
