隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

我们尊重你的安静，也尊重你的隐私

一休冥想无需注册账号，不投放广告，也不出售或出租你的个人信息。可选的使用统计由 Google Analytics / Firebase 处理，具体如下。

数据与设备权限

  • 语言、音量、定时、播放偏好与收藏仅保存在你的设备上。
  • 应用不会读取你的位置、通讯录、照片、健康数据或麦克风内容。
  • 应用不要求登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码或支付信息。

声音与网络

一休冥想播放自然环境声音。部分内容可能通过网络加载，网络服务商可能按其自身政策处理必要的连接日志。我们不会用这些连接建立用户画像。

可选的使用统计

只有在你允许后，网页版才使用 Google Analytics 统计页面访问、声音选择、实际播放时长、播放错误、收藏操作、静心练习完成及会员入口等事件。支持此功能的 App 版本通过 Firebase Analytics 采用相同原则。统计包含随机浏览器或安装标识、设备与系统类别及粗略地区，用于了解回访和汇总趋势；不会上传姓名、联系方式、录音、精确位置或既有练习手记。

你可以在“我的—帮助改进一休”随时关闭后续统计。关闭后不再发送使用事件；这不会自动删除此前已汇总的数据。我们禁用广告个性化和 Google Signals，App 不采集 IDFA 或 IDFV，不将网页和 App 行为关联为同一个人的身份。收集数据由 Google 处理，可能涉及你所在地区以外的服务器，适用 Google 隐私政策。

会员购买由 Apple 处理。购买点击与验证成功分开统计；不会将点击视为收入。运营看板只展示汇总数据，不公开设备标识或个人使用轨迹。需要咨询数据或删除请求时，请通过下方邮箱联系。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集个人信息，也没有用于识别或联系儿童的功能。

YIXIU · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Updated September 30, 2026

Your quiet and your privacy matter

Yixiu requires no account and includes no advertising. We do not sell or rent personal information. Optional usage analytics is processed by Google Analytics / Firebase as described below.

Data and device access

  • Language, volume, timer, playback preferences, and favorites stay on your device.
  • Yixiu does not access your location, contacts, photos, health data, or microphone recordings.
  • No sign-in is required, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment information.

Audio and network use

Yixiu plays nature ambience. Some content may load over the internet, and network providers may process essential connection logs under their own policies. We do not use those connections to create user profiles.

Optional usage analytics

Only after you opt in, the website uses Google Analytics to measure visits, sound choices, actual listening time, playback errors, favorite actions, practice completion and membership interactions. App versions supporting this feature use Firebase Analytics on the same basis. Data includes a random browser or installation identifier, device and system categories and coarse region to understand return visits and aggregate trends. Names, contact information, recordings, precise location and existing journal history are not uploaded.

Turn off future collection anytime in Me → Help improve Yixiu. This does not automatically delete previously aggregated data. Google Signals and ad personalization are disabled; the app collects neither IDFA nor IDFV. Website and app activity are not linked to a shared personal identity. Google processes the data, potentially on servers outside your region, under its privacy policy.

Apple handles payments. Purchase clicks and verified transactions are measured separately; clicks are not revenue. The operations dashboard publishes aggregate results, never device identifiers or individual histories. Contact us below for data questions or deletion requests.

Children's privacy

Yixiu does not knowingly collect personal information from children and includes no feature designed to identify or contact them.
