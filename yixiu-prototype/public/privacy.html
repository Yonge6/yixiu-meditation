隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 10 日

我们尊重你的安静，也尊重你的隐私

一休冥想无需注册账号，不投放广告，也不提供社交或用户内容功能。我们不会出售、出租或分享你的个人信息。

数据与设备权限

  • 语言、音量、定时、播放偏好与收藏仅保存在你的设备上。
  • 应用不会读取你的位置、通讯录、照片、健康数据或麦克风内容。
  • 应用不要求登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码或支付信息。

声音与网络

一休冥想播放自然环境声音。部分内容可能通过网络加载，网络服务商可能按其自身政策处理必要的连接日志。我们不会用这些连接建立用户画像。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集个人信息，也没有用于识别或联系儿童的功能。

YIXIU · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Your quiet and your privacy matter

Yixiu requires no account, includes no advertising, and has no social or user-generated content features. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Data and device access

  • Language, volume, timer, playback preferences, and favorites stay on your device.
  • Yixiu does not access your location, contacts, photos, health data, or microphone recordings.
  • No sign-in is required, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment information.

Audio and network use

Yixiu plays nature ambience. Some content may load over the internet, and network providers may process essential connection logs under their own policies. We do not use those connections to create user profiles.

Children's privacy

Yixiu does not knowingly collect personal information from children and includes no feature designed to identify or contact them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hustyy986@gmail.com. Any changes will be published on this page.
