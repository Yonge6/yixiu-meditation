A simple masking setup

Let the waterfall hold the edge of the room

Noise masking is not about making the waterfall loud. It is about adding one stable layer, choosing one task and leaving the sound alone.

  1. 01

    Notice the distracting sound

    Is it conversation, footsteps, traffic or an irregular appliance? Name the interruption before choosing the background.

  2. 02

    Raise the waterfall slowly

    Stop when the room feels more even. If you are following individual splashes, the level is probably higher than the task needs.

  3. 03

    Set one finish line

    Use a 15- or 30-minute interval for the first block. The timer carries the boundary so you do not need to keep checking it.

Before you begin

Waterfall sounds: common questions

What does noise masking mean?

Noise masking adds a steady background layer so nearby conversations and irregular room sounds stand out less. It does not remove sound or replace hearing protection.

Is this waterfall mixed with music or talking?

No. The preview uses continuous waterfall ambience without music, lyrics or spoken guidance.

When should I choose a waterfall instead of a stream?

Choose the waterfall when you want a fuller, denser texture around a busy room. Choose the stream when you prefer lighter movement and more space between details.

Can the waterfall keep playing in the background on iPhone?

Yes. With background playback enabled in Yixiu, the selected sound can continue while you read, write or use another iPhone app.
