Black-screen rain sounds: common questions

Are these black-screen rain sounds free and ad-free?

Yes. Start the free Window Rain player with no account or ads, then choose Black Screen to cover the open page with a black surface while the timer keeps running. Tap or press Escape to return. For physical iPhone lock-screen playback, continue in Yixiu.

Can rain sounds keep playing when my iPhone screen is locked?

Yes. Turn on background playback in the Yixiu iPhone app and the selected nature sound can continue while the screen is locked or you use another app.

Does Yixiu include a sleep timer?

Yes. Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Yixiu Plus also includes unlimited listening.

Should I choose rain or ocean sounds for sleep?

Choose the texture that feels least demanding to you. Window Rain is soft and even; Ocean Waves have a slower rise and fall. You can try both in the free web player.

Do I need an account or watch ads?

No. Yixiu does not require an account and does not show ads. Your language, volume, timer and favorites remain on your device.
