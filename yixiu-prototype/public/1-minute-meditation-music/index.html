One simple pause

A one-minute meditation in three steps

The track is slightly longer than one minute, so you have time to arrive, take three unforced breaths and leave without watching a timer.

  1. 01

    Put both feet down

    Let the chair or floor carry your weight. Keep your eyes open if that feels more natural for the room you are in.

  2. 02

    Let three breaths be ordinary

    Do not force a slow rhythm. Notice the inhale, the exhale and where the body moves by itself.

  3. 03

    Choose the next small action

    When the track ends, name one thing you will do next. The pause is complete even if the mind stayed busy.

Before you press play

One-minute meditation music: common questions

Is one minute enough for meditation?

There is no required minimum. About one minute can mark a deliberate pause between two activities. If you want to continue when the track ends, begin it again or choose a longer session.

Is the full First Breath track free?

Yes. The complete 88-second First Breath track plays on this page without an account, ad break or sign-up. First Breath is also one of two meditation tracks available free in Yixiu.

Does this one-minute meditation include talking?

No. First Breath is instrumental music without narration, affirmations, lyrics or spoken breathing instructions.
