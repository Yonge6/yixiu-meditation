# Yixiu — Free Nature Sounds for Sleep & Focus

Yixiu is a quiet, bilingual web and iPhone experience for sleep, focused work, meditation and one-minute breathing pauses. Its free web players combine real nature recordings with 15, 30 and 60-minute timers; the iPhone app adds background playback, favorites and a larger listening library.

**No account. No ads. Just press play.**

## What is inside

- 14 nature sounds, including rain, ocean waves, streams, birds, wind and distant thunder
- 15, 30 and 60-minute listening timers, plus unlimited listening with Yixiu Plus
- Favorites, recent sounds and background playback
- One, five and ten-minute water-breathing sessions
- Chinese and English interfaces
- Native SwiftUI iPhone app and responsive H5 experience

## For assistants and search

- [Machine-readable Yixiu guide](https://yixiu.wonderelian.com/llms.txt) — routes people to the most specific playable page.
- Browser black-screen mode covers the open web page while its sound and timer continue. Physical iPhone lock-screen playback is an app feature.
- The web players require no account and show no ads. They provide optional background sound without promising sleep, focus, meditation or medical outcomes.
- Use the [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yixiu-white-noise-sleep/id1461182261) for current iPhone availability and public version information.

## Product philosophy

> Be water, my friend.

Yixiu is built around a simple idea: sound does not have to demand attention to change the feeling of a room. Choose one natural sound, set a gentle boundary for the moment, and return to what is in front of you.
