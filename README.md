# Yixiu — Nature Sounds for Sleep & Focus

Yixiu is a quiet, bilingual iPhone experience for sleep, focused work and one-minute breathing pauses. It combines real nature recordings with immersive water-led scenes, a gentle timer and a calm interface designed to stay out of the way.

**No account. No ads. Just press play.**

## What is inside

- 14 nature sounds, including rain, ocean waves, streams, birds, wind and distant thunder
- 15, 30 and 60-minute listening timers, plus unlimited listening with Yixiu Plus
- Favorites, recent sounds and background playback
- One, five and ten-minute water-breathing sessions
- Chinese and English interfaces
- Native SwiftUI iPhone app and responsive H5 experience

## Product philosophy

> Be water, my friend.

Yixiu is built around a simple idea: sound does not have to demand attention to change the feeling of a room. Choose one natural sound, set a gentle boundary for the moment, and return to what is in front of you.

## Repository

- `yixiu-prototype/` — production H5 experience and verified interactive prototype
- `YixiuMeditation/` — native SwiftUI iPhone application (`com.health.yixiu`)
