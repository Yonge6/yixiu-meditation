## Composition and dimensions

Native UI pixels are proportionally resized, never non-proportionally stretched or redrawn. Rounded masks and shadows belong to the marketing layout, not to the app interface. New localized poster subjects are everyday rest, one-minute breathing, quiet nighttime listening, classical music, AI-assisted originals and finding favorite sounds. Copy does not promise treatment, guaranteed sleep, research-proven outcomes, or unimplemented Plus benefits.

Twenty-four uploaded/processed assets: 12 Duo posters (1398 × 2034), six iPad posters (2064 × 2752), two retained-widget compositions (416 × 496), two product-page headers (3840 × 1646 PNG), and two search creatives (3840 × 2560 JPEG). All final dimensions and opaque output formats are verified against Apple's processed records in `asset-manifest.json`.

Only draft associations for uploaded groups are replaced. Original library images, ordinary-iPhone draft media and the live 1.20 version remain recoverable/unchanged. There is no App Review submission or approval claim until the actual submission flow/readback succeeds.

References checked: Apple's product-page guidance, screenshot specifications and creative-asset best practices. Metadata length limits: name/subtitle 30, keywords 100, promotional text 170, description 4000 characters; all proposed text passes.
