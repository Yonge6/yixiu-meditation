### Description

Yixiu is a quiet, ad-free nature sound app for sleep, focus, reading, and brief breathing resets. Choose from 14 real soundscapes—including ocean waves, rain, streams, birds, thunder, and wind—set a gentle timer, or follow a one-minute water-breathing rhythm. No account, no ads, and no streaks. Listen free on the web or continue on iPhone in English or Chinese.

### First maker comment

Hi Product Hunt — I’m Elian, the maker of Yixiu.

I built Yixiu because many wellness apps add another account, feed, streak, or decision to a moment when you already want less noise.

Yixiu keeps the interaction small:

- 14 real nature sounds for sleep, focus, and reading
- a gentle 15, 30, or 60-minute timer
- a one-minute water-breathing reset
- no account, no ads, and full English and Chinese support

The web version lets you listen before downloading. The iPhone app adds background playback and keeps preferences on your device. Version 1.4 is the first fully bilingual release.

I’d especially value feedback on one question: when you need to reset, do you reach first for sound, a timer, or a breathing cue?
