## Changes

- Focus offers Rain on Eaves / 15 minutes, Stream breathing / 1 minute, and Morning Birds / 5 minutes. These scenes remain free; the new five-minute listening timer is free, while existing Plus gates remain enforced.
- Native playback and breathing use elapsed-time deadlines, preserve fractional pause time, reset when reselecting a duration, and reconcile delayed callbacks. Focus ambience does not double-count a completed listening practice.
- Me shows Monday–Sunday completed activity, this week's completed minutes and the latest three practices with replay. Stores at most 200 completed records locally; no partial-session or historical backfill.
- H5 decodes records defensively, removes invalid/duplicate entries and remains usable when storage is unavailable, with an explicit persistence warning.
- UI reuses the existing water photography and serif/sans pair: photographic practice rows, restrained aqua controls, refined spacing and a quiet editorial journal panel. Sounds remains immersive.
- Daily notification taps route to a ready one-minute Focus practice without unexpected audio. Medium widgets retain the existing breathing action and add a localized link back to the saved sound. `yixiu://focus` and `yixiu://resume` are app-owned routes.
- Native version/build is 1.10 (23). Public Release does not force Plus. Internal Plus continues to require both DEBUG and YIXIU_INTERNAL_PLUS.
