### Review notes (append to existing accurate app details)

Version 1.11, build 24 updates adaptive layouts using standard SwiftUI window
geometry and AnyLayout. No private folding APIs or device-name assumptions are
used. Rotate an iPhone or resize an iPad window: Sounds and Focus change between
stacked and side-by-side layouts where space permits. An active listening or
breathing session retains its state across layout changes. All content remains
scrollable in smaller windows. The timer sheet offers 5/15/30-minute free
listening and verified Plus access to 60-minute/unlimited listening.

No account is required. Purchases use Apple In-App Purchase with StoreKit
verification. Free, legacy and Plus policies are unchanged from 1.10. Restore
Purchases is available in My Yixiu. This formal Release build must not contain
the DEBUG-only YIXIU_INTERNAL_PLUS entitlement override.

Screenshots will show actual current iPhone/iPad simulator interfaces, not a
fabricated Duo device or simulated Apple endorsement. Device-specific Duo
testing remains pending availability of the official simulator/hardware.
