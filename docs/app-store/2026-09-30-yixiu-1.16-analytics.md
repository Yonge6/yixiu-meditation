## Privacy

- Default off; explicit Me > Help improve Yixiu consent; withdrawal disables collection and resets Analytics local data.
- Only verified production StoreKit installs initialize Firebase. Debug/internal Plus, simulator, TestFlight and sandbox remain excluded.
- Five analytics data categories: random app-installation identifier (Device ID), network-derived coarse location, product interaction, purchase history, and other diagnostics (fixed playback-error codes). All are conservatively marked linked via the installation identifier, not advertising tracking.
- No account identity, IDFA/IDFV, journal history, voice recordings or precise location. No Crashlytics or advertising SDK product is selected.
- Native privacy explanation expanded to cover coarse region, purchase/subscription events, errors, and withdrawal limits. Bundled main-app privacy manifest added; UserDefaults only reads/writes app-local preferences (CA92.1).
- Removed system-uptime use from listening analytics; wall time only bounds confirmed audio progress. Clock-change regression proves a clock adjustment cannot invent listening time.

Official sources checked: [Google Analytics disclosure](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10285841?hl=en), [Firebase SDK disclosure](https://firebase.google.com/docs/ios/app-store-data-collection), [Apple privacy definitions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).

Apple's five-category privacy declaration was completed and published on September 30. A fresh page reload and the detailed product-page preview confirmed product interaction, Device ID, coarse location, purchase history and other diagnostics, all Analytics / linked / not tracking. This replaces the previous Data Not Collected declaration in preparation for the analytics release.

Chinese and English promotional text, descriptions and What's New have been saved for 1.16. Review notes explain default-off consent, withdrawal, production-only StoreKit validation, excluded test environments and unchanged pricing/entitlements. Automatic release after approval, immediate rollout and existing ratings are preserved. Existing 1.15 screenshots are retained.
