# Rain Lock-Screen Pinterest Pin Design

## Goal

Create one useful, search-shaped Pinterest Pin that sends qualified visitors to Yixiu's new iPhone lock-screen rain guide. Publish only to the dedicated `Yixiu: Nature Sounds & Sleep` board and keep all destination, content and evidence inside the Yixiu project.

## Directions considered

1. **Troubleshooting card — selected.** Lead with `Rain stops when your iPhone locks?`, show the Apple settings path and offer the full guide plus a real rain preview. This matches a concrete problem and is distinct from the existing rain-for-sleep Pins.
2. **Atmospheric rain card.** Visually calm, but too similar to Yixiu's existing Window Rain distribution and less likely to match troubleshooting searches.
3. **Apple versus Yixiu comparison.** Potentially informative, but busier, more promotional and less direct than answering the setting question first.

## Visual and copy

- Format: 1000 × 1500 PNG, Pinterest 2:3.
- Source: existing Yixiu Window Rain WebP; no new outside asset.
- Treatment: dark navy rain background, cyan-white typography and a simple drawn lock icon.
- Hook: `RAIN STOPS WHEN YOUR IPHONE LOCKS?`
- Utility: `Settings → Accessibility → Audio & Visual → Background Sounds` and `Turn off “Stop Sounds When Locked”`.
- CTA: `STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE + FREE RAIN PREVIEW`.
- Title: `Rain Stops When Your iPhone Locks? Check This Setting`.
- Description: explain the Apple setting first, then mention Yixiu's real rain preview and 15/30/60-minute timer without promising a sleep result.

## Attribution and proof

- Destination: `/rain-sounds-when-iphone-locked/` with `utm_source=pinterest`, `utm_medium=organic_share`, `utm_campaign=iphone_background_audio` and `utm_content=rain_lock_screen_pin_01`.
- Public proof requires a Pinterest Pin URL, WonderElian account identity, exact Yixiu board, visible title, destination readback and HTTP 200 from both the Pin page and target.
- The Pin is one distribution experiment, not proof of traffic or downloads. The completed-day GA4 100-UV gate remains separate.
