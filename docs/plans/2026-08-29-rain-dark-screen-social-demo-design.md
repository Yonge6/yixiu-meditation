# Rain Dark-Screen Social Demo Design

## Goal and constraints

Create one truthful 20-second Yixiu product demonstration that turns the newly released rain dark-screen player into an external acquisition surface. Publish the same core demonstration natively on the official WonderElian Instagram and Pinterest accounts, with platform-specific calls to action. Every destination must remain on `yixiu.wonderelian.com` or the existing Yixiu App Store page. Do not touch, mention or route through another product; do not upload to YouTube, X or Facebook.

## Options considered

1. Publish another ordinary Pinterest image. This provides a direct link but the board already has 40 Pins and today's GA4 snapshot contains only one Pinterest active user.
2. Edit the caption of the existing 110-view Rain Window Reel. This improves conversion wording but creates no fresh distribution.
3. Publish a new feature-led product demo to Instagram Reels and Pinterest video. This combines Instagram's stronger observed view surface with Pinterest's direct destination link and gives both platforms a new, concrete reason to distribute the content.

Choose option 3. The creative begins with the real rain-window visual and the question `Need rain without a bright screen?`, demonstrates `Play rain`, `Tap Darken Screen`, and `Timer keeps running`, then becomes a true black screen with a quiet Yixiu label. The same real `light-rain.m4a` recording runs throughout. The Instagram ending says `LINK IN BIO · OPEN SLEEP SOUNDS`; the Pinterest ending says `TAP VISIT SITE` and uses a direct attributed Sleep Sounds URL.

## Verification

Export 1080×1920 H.264/AAC at 30 fps for 20 seconds. Confirm the source audio is present, inspect representative opening, transition and ending frames, and calculate SHA256. After publication, require permanent public URLs, correct WonderElian authorship, exact copy and destination readback, and platform disclosure settings. Publication alone does not prove a visit or download; refresh exact-hostname GA4 afterward and preserve unknown Apple metrics as `null`.
