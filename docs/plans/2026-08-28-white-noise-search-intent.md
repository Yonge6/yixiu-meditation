# Yixiu Deep White Noise Search Experiment

## Decision

Strengthen the existing `/underwater-white-noise-for-sleep/` page instead of creating a second white-noise URL. A live site-restricted search surfaced Yixiu's ocean and rain pages but not the underwater white-noise page. The page already has a truthful first-party recording, an attributed App Store route and four aligned FAQ answers, so a second page would split authority without adding a distinct listening experience.

The selected experiment moves the broad, high-intent phrase `deep white noise for sleeping` to the beginning of the title and H1 while retaining `underwater` as the sound's honest differentiator. It also brings the proven 15, 30 and 60-minute browser timer to this page and serves a compressed WebP hero with the PNG retained for social previews and fallback.

## Measurement boundary

The page will emit the existing preview and App Store events plus the shared timer-selection and timer-completion events. Success is increased verified organic landing traffic and attributed App Store action, not a ranking claim. The overall goal remains a completed Beijing natural day with at least 100 GA4 active users and official Apple download evidence.
