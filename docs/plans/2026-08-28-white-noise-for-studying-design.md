# White Noise for Studying landing page design

## Goal

Capture the English search intent `white noise for studying` with a fast, useful Yixiu-owned page that lets the visitor hear the matching sound immediately and then continue in the iPhone app.

## Evidence and positioning

- Current search results favor pages with an immediate browser player, a clear no-lyrics promise, and practical guidance about masking an unpredictable study room.
- Yixiu already owns a licensed `underwater-white-noise.m4a` scene categorized for deep focus, so the page can keep the search promise without inventing or substituting audio.
- The page must not claim universal focus, memory, ADHD, or medical benefits. It should recommend a low volume, one real task, and a short comparison against silence.

## Experience

1. Search-aligned title, description, canonical URL, social metadata, and JSON-LD.
2. One-tap preview of the real Underwater Echo recording.
3. A 15/30/60-minute web timer so the page itself solves the immediate job.
4. Focus-campaign App Store links before and after playback, with Yixiu analytics placements.
5. Internal links from Guides and adjacent study pages; reciprocal links to rain, river, and sleep white-noise pages.

## Acceptance

- One H1; title 50–60 characters; description 150–160 characters.
- Unique canonical and matching WebPage/ImageObject/SoftwareApplication/FAQPage schema.
- Real audio and existing optimized hero asset load successfully.
- No unsupported rating, guarantee, treatment, cure, ADHD, or universal-performance claims.
- Page appears in the Guides ItemList and sitemap, and is covered by static tests and production deployment checks.
- Mobile 390px runtime: no horizontal overflow; preview plays; timer and post-play download CTA work.
