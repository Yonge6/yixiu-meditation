# Forest Sleep Pinterest Pin

## Goal

Create one searchable, click-through Pinterest Pin for Yixiu's live Forest Sounds for Sleep page. The Pin must stay on the dedicated Yixiu board and use an attributed destination URL.

## Options considered

- Static Pinterest Pin: fastest durable, clickable discovery asset and easiest to attribute.
- Pinterest video Pin: potentially more reach, but slower to produce and validate.
- Instagram image post: useful for awareness, but the caption destination is not clickable.

The static Pinterest Pin is the first release. A video variant can follow only if the static asset or page earns useful signal.

## Chosen direction

- 1000 x 1500 portrait image using Yixiu's existing mountain-valley artwork.
- Dark forest-green gradients preserve legibility while keeping the landscape visible.
- Search intent appears in the main title: `FOREST SOUNDS FOR SLEEP`.
- Product truth appears directly below it: `WIND · DISTANT BIRDS · NO MUSIC`.
- The free web timer is the concrete CTA: `FREE 15 · 30 · 60 MIN TIMER`.
- Footer identifies Yixiu and `yixiu.wonderelian.com`.

## Publishing metadata

- Board: `Yixiu: Nature Sounds & Sleep`
- Title: `Forest Sounds for Sleep — Free 15/30/60 Min Timer`
- Destination: `https://yixiu.wonderelian.com/forest-sounds-for-sleep/?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic_share&utm_campaign=sleep_sounds&utm_content=forest_sleep_pin_01`
- AI disclosure: enabled because the source landscape is AI-generated or AI-modified.
- Similar-product recommendations: disabled when the control is available.

## Proof gates

- Public Pin URL returns successfully.
- Public page identifies the WonderElian account and the dedicated Yixiu board.
- Title, description, destination, and image are visible on the public Pin.
- Destination resolves to the live Yixiu page and preserves the UTM parameters.
- The Pin is not counted as traffic or a download; those outcomes require GA4 and Apple evidence.
