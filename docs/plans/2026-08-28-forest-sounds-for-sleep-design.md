# Forest Sounds for Sleep Design

## Outcome

Create a distinct English acquisition page at `/forest-sounds-for-sleep/` for people who explicitly want forest ambience at bedtime. The page must let visitors hear Yixiu's real forest-breeze recording immediately, set a 15, 30 or 60-minute web preview timer, and reach the matched Sleep App Store custom product page through the shared Apple campaign attribution.

## Search intent and boundaries

- Primary intent: listen to forest sounds for sleep without music or talking.
- Secondary questions: what is audible, whether birds are prominent, how to set a low-volume bedtime session, and how forest ambience differs from rain, ocean waves or mountain wind.
- Keep the page separate from `/forest-sounds-for-focus/`: the sleep page is about winding down, low volume, a defined ending and screen-dark/background playback; the focus page remains about reading and daytime work.
- Do not promise improved sleep, treatment, noise-blocking performance or universal results.

## Experience

The hero leads with one-tap playback of `forest-breeze.m4a`, followed by the existing 15/30/60-minute preview timer and a Sleep custom-product-page CTA. Supporting sections explain a restrained bedtime setup, compare forest ambience with three other real Yixiu textures, and answer four visible FAQ questions that match the structured data exactly.

## Content and linking

- Primary page: `/forest-sounds-for-sleep/`
- Incoming links: Guides, Rain Sounds for Sleeping, and Forest Sounds for Focus.
- Related links from the new page: Rain Sounds for Sleeping, Wind Sounds for Sleeping, Ocean Waves for Sleeping, Forest Sounds for Focus, and Guides.
- App Store custom product page: Sleep (`67cb8784-2b16-4849-b940-90fdf4d99752`).
- Analytics placements: `forest_sleep_preview`, `forest_sleep_landing`, and `forest_sleep_after_preview`.

## Evidence gates

- One H1, truthful title and 150-160 character meta description.
- WebPage, ImageObject, SoftwareApplication and FAQPage schema aligned with visible content.
- Real first-party audio and existing 941x1672 hero image.
- Shared Apple campaign parameters on every App Store action.
- Sitemap, Guides ItemList/card, contextual inbound links and deployment checks updated.
- Static tests, build, Sites tests, runtime tests, 390px overflow check, audio playback, timer and post-play CTA acceptance before deployment.
