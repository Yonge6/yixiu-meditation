# Yixiu bright meditation scenes

## Goal

Replace five 2:3 portrait meditation covers that read too dark in the iPhone library and full-screen player. The new set must feel like one Yixiu family while making each place recognizable at card size. No text, logos, UI, people, buildings, or product mockups appear inside the art.

## Shared art direction

- 1024 x 1536 portrait composition, premium cinematic nature photography with a restrained painterly finish.
- Brighter midtones, clean luminous air, aqua and mineral-blue shadows, warm ivory or pale-gold light; no crushed blacks or heavy navy wash.
- Keep the main subject and horizon inside the central 70% so SwiftUI `scaledToFill` remains readable on both the library card and full-screen player.
- Preserve quietness: soft movement, generous negative space, natural detail, no fantasy objects or aggressive saturation.

## Scene briefs

1. **Still Water / 静水** — a glass-calm alpine lake at luminous early morning, pearl sky and soft sun, clear mountain reflection, pale aqua water. Serenity comes from symmetry and stillness rather than darkness.
2. **Open Meadow / 原野舒展** — sunlit grassland after rain, a narrow bright stream leading inward, fresh green and soft wildflowers, open blue sky, warm morning rays. Spacious and gently energizing.
3. **Oasis Rest / 绿洲停歇** — welcoming turquoise spring among warm sand and date palms, late-afternoon sun, visible foliage and water detail, sheltered and restorative rather than nocturnal.
4. **Ocean Passage / 海上行旅** — bright open sea at golden sunrise, a subtle wake or luminous path crossing the water toward the horizon, airy clouds and hopeful forward motion; calm, not stormy.
5. **Quiet Orbit / 静默星轨** — serene blue-hour lake with unmistakable long-exposure star-trail arcs circling a visible celestial pole across the upper half. The trails must remain prominent in a small crop, reflected faintly in the water, while the landscape retains readable blue midtones.

## Acceptance

Each source is independently generated in ChatGPT through desktop Chrome, downloaded at the largest available size, normalized to sRGB 1024 x 1536 JPEG, and visually checked in both the grid card and full-screen player. Quiet Orbit fails acceptance if the star trails are not obvious without zooming.
