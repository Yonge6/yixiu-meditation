## App Review notes

Yixiu requires no account and contains no ads or user-generated content. All paid access uses Apple In-App Purchase only. The app is for relaxation and mindfulness and makes no medical diagnosis or treatment claims.

Build 18 updates the tab navigation, scene artwork, Plus badge, and language control. To review free content, launch the app and play any item marked FREE. To review Plus, open the meditation library, choose a gemstone-marked item, and use the monthly or yearly Apple subscription option. Restore Purchases is available from My Yixiu.
